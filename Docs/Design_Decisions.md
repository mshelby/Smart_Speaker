# Design Decisions

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Core Philosophy
The Mark Shelby Design Philosophy emphasizes honest engineering, quiet luxury, and timeless minimalism. Inspired by clean functional design, the BarrelSat One prioritizes acoustic performance, serviceability, and a refined "old money" aesthetic.

## Key Decisions
- Barrel form with 200 mm diameter and ~175 mm height
- 2.5 mm nominal wall thickness (1.8 mm at speaker areas)
- 4.5 mm spherical dimples with 7 mm vertical spacing
- 40 × 5.5 mm speaker holes on top plate
- Modular top and bottom with magnetic closure and TPU gaskets
- Opposing Dayton PC83-4 woofers + upward ND25FA-4 tweeter
- Internal volume target ~2.7–2.85 liters

All decisions prioritize the balance of premium appearance, printability, and sound quality.
